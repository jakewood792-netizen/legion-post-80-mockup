Why Join Post 80

The American Legion Post 80 in New Richmond, WI welcomes veterans and their families to join a community built on camaraderie, service, and support. Membership connects you with fellow veterans while helping fund the programs Post 80 runs for veterans, families, and youth in the area.

  • Camaraderie with fellow veterans in the New Richmond area
  • Member discounts through The American Legion's national partner network
  • A subscription to The American Legion Magazine, a top-rated publication for members
  • A direct hand in funding local veterans assistance, youth mentoring, and community programs
