Welcome to Post 80

The American Legion Post 80, also known as Butler-Harmon Post 80, has served veterans and the New Richmond community for generations. Whether you're a veteran looking to connect, a neighbor coming out for Bingo night, or a family planning an event in our hall, there's a place for you here.
